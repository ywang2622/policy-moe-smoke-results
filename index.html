What is complete?

All 125 questions include source evidence, unique IDs, and validated formats. The held-out set contains 10 Level 1, 10 Level 2, and 5 Level 3 questions. Two open-ended test items include five-point judging rubrics. All 18 automated pipeline checks pass.

What comes next?

Train the frozen Qwen baseline comparison, the single shared LoRA, three level-specialist LoRAs, and the learned linear top-1 router on the 100-question split; then evaluate all systems on the same 25 held-out questions.

What happened?

The shared LoRA answered one of two Level 1 questions correctly; the other systems missed both. Every system answered the two Level 2 items and the objective Level 3 item correctly. With counts this small, the responsible conclusion is simply that training and inference completed—not that one architecture is better.

From documents to answers

What we actually built

The raw source format and core three-expert idea follow the paper’s official release. The questions and implementation are our own pilot—not a reproduction of the paper’s published results.

  1. 01

    Analyze policy documents

    Twenty-five DOE documents from the paper repository’s released policy archive.

  2. 02

    Author three-level questions

    Facts, understanding, and applied scenarios; 125 total, grounded in source evidence.

  3. 03

    Split without document leakage

    Twenty documents for training and five entire documents held out for testing.

  4. 04

    Train adapters and router

    One shared LoRA, three specialist LoRAs, and a learned linear top-1 router.

  5. 05

    Compare consistently

    Frozen Qwen, shared LoRA, and PolicyMoE will use the same 25 held-out questions.

Known limitations

What this does not prove

  • No statistical significance or architecture superiority.
  • No replication of the paper’s full benchmark or published results.
  • The 125-question dataset has not yet completed GPU training.
  • No two-judge score for the open Level 3 response yet.
  • Questions were authored and editorially reviewed, not generated in a logged live model run.
  • Distractor traces are reconstructed fixtures, not original generation logs.
